About the firm

Named for a bird that hunts alone.

A kestrel hovers in place, reads the ground below without moving, and commits only when it's certain. We built a firm around that discipline — small, watchful, and unwilling to act before the picture is clear.

Founded 2011

We started by refusing the staffing model.

Kestrel was founded by three partners who left larger firms over the same disagreement: the person who scoped an engagement was rarely the person doing the work. Clients paid partner rates and got a rotating team of analysts two years out of a degree.

So the firm was built with a rule that hasn't moved since — the partner who takes the first call is the partner in the room on the last day of the engagement. It caps how much work we can carry. We've kept the rule anyway.

What governs the work

Four commitments, held regardless of client size.

One partner, start to finish

No handoff between the pitch and the delivery team. The person who diagnosed the problem defends the recommendation.

Six engagements, firm-wide

A hard cap, not a target. We turn away work in a strong year rather than dilute attention in a weak one.

Written positions, not decks

Every recommendation is delivered as a memo a board can read before the meeting, not slides that only make sense narrated.

Fixed fee, scoped upfront

No hourly billing. The fee is set against a defined deliverable before the engagement begins, and it doesn't move.

We stay past the decision

Engagements include a standing quarter of follow-through after the recommendation is adopted, at no additional fee.

No public case studies

What we learn inside a client's operation stays there. References are made by direct introduction only.
