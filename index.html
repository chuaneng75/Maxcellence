The position

We are hired for the decision no one else wants to own.

Most advisory work is diagnostic — it tells a board what it already suspects, in a heavier binder. Ours ends in a recommendation with a name attached to it. If a client needs someone to sit at the table on the day the decision gets made, that is the work we take.

01 — Diagnose
